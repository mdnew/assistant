# Idea: email announcing the Election Center in the app

**Captured:** September 9, 2026
**Status:** Idea, not committed
**Owner:** Matt
**Related:** `meetings/actions/2026-08-26-kqed-election-2026.md`, `meetings/notes/2026-08-25-kqed-voter-guide.md`

## The idea

Send **one email** telling KQED's audience the Election Center exists in the app, and use it as the re-engagement send for the month.

Matt's stated goal is one re-engagement email this month, with the caveat that it may be too ambitious given KQED team bandwidth. This is the version of that email with a built-in reason to exist: there is a real, dated, newsworthy thing to announce rather than a generic "come back" message.

## Why this one email instead of a separate re-engagement send

- **It has news value.** An election hub with a voter guide, radio clips, and live results is a reason to open the app, not just a nudge
- **The audience already exists.** The Aug 25 voter guide session identified users who have "local politics" selected in Personalize but push disabled. That is a re-engagement list with demonstrated interest
- **It feeds a target already set.** Election week goals include a notification opt-in rate toward 25%. An email that drives app opens and opt-ins serves that directly
- **It stacks with work already scheduled** rather than adding a parallel track: elections push campaign starting October, kqed.org slide-in refresh in October, Election Alerts opt-in (Matt's workstream)

## Timing

- Election day is **November 3**. Voter registration and ballot return deadlines land before it
- The elections-specific push campaign and slide-in updates are both slated for **October**
- Sending ahead of or alongside the October campaign start gives the opt-in list time to build before election week

Open question: send in **September** (Matt's stated goal, earlier reach, but the Election Center may not be built enough to show) or **early October** (more to announce, aligns with the campaign, misses the "this month" goal).

## What to point at

Depends what actually ships. Priority order from the Aug 28 session is voter guide first, then clips/hub, results last:

- Voter guide, in-app rather than kicking out to the browser
- Latest election news playlist from radio coverage
- Election alerts opt-in (the toggle Matt is building)
- Ballot scratch pad, if it makes the cut
- Live results on election night
