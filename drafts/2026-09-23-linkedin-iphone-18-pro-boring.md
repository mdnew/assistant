The iPhone 18 Pro is the least interesting phone Apple has shipped in years. I think that was on purpose.

Here is the list. A variable aperture on the main camera. A Dynamic Island that can hold three things at once instead of one. A faster chip. Siri AI arriving in beta. In October. Again.

And a hundred dollars more than last year.

Put it next to a 17 Pro and most people cannot finish the sentence that starts "I should upgrade because."

The part I keep chewing on is that nobody at Apple got this wrong. The foldable was the launch this year. Someone decided the Pro's job this cycle was to hold the line and not lose anyone, and that is a real strategy. Mature products earn the right to have quiet years. Most roadmaps would be better if more teams took one.

But a quiet year and a price increase are hard to run at the same time. "Not much changed" and "that will be a hundred dollars more" is the one combination your customers will actually notice, because you asked them to notice it.

If you are going to take a boring year, take the boring year. The bill is what turns restraint into a question.

When your flagship goes quiet for a cycle, is that discipline or is it drift?
