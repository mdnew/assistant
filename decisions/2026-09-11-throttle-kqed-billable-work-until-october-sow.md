# Decision: Throttle Matt's billable KQED work until the October SOW

**Date**: 2026-09-11
**Status**: decided
**Made by**: Matt New

## Context

Uptech is **running hot on the current KQED budget**. The replacement engagement is already in motion: two deals sit at Proposal (In Progress) 40%, **KQED - 2026/2027 Mobile Development ($600K)** and **KQED - 2026/2027 Web Development ($700K)**, both with an **Oct 1, 2026** close date. Julia has approved the six-bullet structure of the Mobile SOW; only the number is outstanding.

Burning through the remaining budget before the new SOW lands would mean either working unpaid or asking KQED for a mid-cycle increase while a $1.3M renewal is on the table.

## Decision

**Matt is intentionally slowing his own billable KQED work until the new SOW starts in October.**

## Alternatives Considered

| Option | Why Rejected |
|--------|-------------|
| Keep working at normal pace and bill it | Exhausts a budget that is already running hot, right as a $1.3M renewal is being negotiated |
| Keep working and absorb the hours unbilled | Same delivery risk, no revenue, and it hides the real cost of the engagement |
| Ask KQED for a mid-cycle budget increase | Poor timing against the renewal conversation; muddies a clean SOW discussion |

## Rationale

The renewal is worth far more than the marginal delivery in September. Protecting the remaining budget keeps the SOW conversation clean and avoids an awkward overage discussion with Julia and Jason while terms are being set.

## Confidence on the renewal

**Matt (Sep 11): the KQED SOWs will happen. It is a question of papering it up, not of winning the work.** The risk here is administrative and calendar-driven, not competitive. Treat the Oct 1 date as a contracting milestone rather than a deal that could be lost.

## Tradeoffs Accepted

- **Matt's utilization looks low through September.** Sep 9 and Sep 10 logged 13.5 hours with **zero billable time**. That is deliberate, not drift
- KQED delivery momentum slows during the gap, including **Election 2026**, which has a hard November deadline. Since the SOW itself is not in doubt, this is a self-imposed delay on work that will certainly be funded
- Other Uptech staff on KQED may still be consuming budget even while Matt throttles

## Expected Consequences

- Matt's KQED time through September is mostly **Non-billable**: SOW work, coordination, internal syncs
- Normal pace resumes when the new SOW starts in **October**
- If the papering slips past Oct 1, revisit quickly. The work is not at risk, but **Election 2026 cannot absorb much delay** regardless of why the calendar moves
